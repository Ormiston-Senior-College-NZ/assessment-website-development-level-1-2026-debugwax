Joining the Indian Diaspora Club

Our club is dedicated to celebrating and sharing the rich culture and heritage of the Indian diaspora. We organize various activities and events throughout the year to promote cultural awareness and foster a sense of community among our members.

If you are interested in joining, take a look at what we have done in Activities and contact us by emailing email@ormistion.school.nz.

This is for students of Ormistion Senior College
